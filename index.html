Quick Tips

  • Always check the domain spelling carefully (e.g. paypa1.com vs paypal.com)
  • Look for HTTPS and a valid padlock icon in the browser
  • Be wary of urgent language, unexpected attachments, or requests for credentials
  • Hover over links before clicking to preview the real destination
  • This tool uses heuristics only — it is not a guarantee of safety
